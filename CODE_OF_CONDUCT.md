# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in AI-DLC v2 Engine respectful, welcoming, and
productive for people of varied backgrounds, identities, abilities,
experiences, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with patience and professional care.
- Critique ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience.
- Give credit and preserve provenance.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's information without
  permission.
- Raise safety, security, and governance concerns constructively.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while using the appeal process when
  needed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or degrading language.
- Sexualized attention or imagery.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or unwanted contact.
- Publishing private information.
- Knowingly introducing credentials, confidential data, or unlicensed
  material.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Disrupting project spaces or review processes in bad faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other
material that violate this code. Responses can include a private warning,
temporary participation limits, removal from a role, or a permanent ban,
proportional to severity and pattern.

Reports should use the private conduct contact configured by the project
operator. Do not include sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse
themselves when appropriate. Reports should be acknowledged promptly, handled
as privately as practical, and documented with the minimum necessary detail.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person is publicly representing
the project.
